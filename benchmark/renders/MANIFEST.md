The `a4` measure copies the MWIT report template in `GOaT-Documents/style.typ`
(`MarginAcademic`). The `narrow` measure is chosen so the 32mm text measure is
roughly one fifth of the academic measure: at 16pt TH Sarabun New a long
unspaced Thai run regularly exceeds 32mm, so overfull lines occur in
`plain` and the emergency-break threshold has something to act on.
